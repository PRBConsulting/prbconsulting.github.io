Excel workbook · download

Payroll reconciliation toolkit

The pre-approval check I run every month, rebuilt from scratch as a generic workbook. It ties what should be paid to what the payroll system calculated, before the money leaves.

  • Expected vs. paid by pay code and by employee, with a rounding tolerance
  • Terminated employees paid, new hires without bank details, net pay swings vs. last period
  • Bank instructions: splits that do not add to 100%, wrong account length, accounts shared between employees
  • Preparer and reviewer checklist that says whether the run is ready to approve

Excel tables, SUMIFS, INDEX/MATCH, SUMPRODUCT · no macros · works with an export from any payroll system · 120 fictitious employees with seeded errors to find
